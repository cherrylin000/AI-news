🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

The Agent Economy Meets the Craft Debate

Today's feed shows the AI stack industrializing fast: token demand compounding ~40% MoM, inference clouds proliferating, and new agent-native infrastructure like message boards emerging. Yet builders push back on pure generation — good software still needs strong, human opinions and deep craft.
Key Points:

1. Compute as Energy: Rauch frames compute/tokens as the new economy's energy unit — durable oil rush, not a gold rush.
2. Agent Infrastructure: vercel board ('headless Slack for agents') targets software factories and decentralized multi-agent systems.
3. Model Frontier: GPT-6.2 Astra showcases hard-task and chip-level (AMD, Jalapeño) optimization gains.
4. Craft Counterweight: Thariq and Ivan Zhao argue good software requires taste, strong opinions, and human judgment.
Implications:

1. Infra Will Consolidate: Expect expansion then consolidation in inference as capex, security, ZDR and regulation bite.
2. Own Your Pitch: Founders should sell ambitious second-order outcomes to stand out in a crowded funding market.
Bottom line: The pipes of AI are being laid at exponential speed, but differentiation increasingly lives in human taste and agent-native infrastructure.

1. 基础设施将整合: 受资本开支、安全、ZDR 与监管影响，推理领域先扩张后整合。
