🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Cheaper Intelligence, Smarter Routing, Domain Trust

Builders are shifting from raw model scale to cost-aware routing, smaller-model control, and domain-native agents. Legal AI shows model upgrades can erase product gaps, while finance faces regulatory scrutiny.
Key Points:

1. Cost-aware intelligence: $200 plans suffice for great work; intelligence price will keep falling.
2. Routing & small models: Per-task model routing and direct weight manipulation are emerging levers.
3. Agent-native vertical SaaS: Legal agents compete on trust and workflow, not just model access.
Implications:

1. Moat shifts: Don't build on temporary model gaps; own workflow, data, and trust.
2. Regulation risk: Finance AI faces enforcement; compliance becomes a differentiator.
Bottom line: The winning stack is cheaper tokens, smarter routing, and domain trust—not just bigger models.

更便宜的智能、更聪明的路由、更深的领域信任

Builder们正从单纯追求模型规模转向成本感知的路由、小模型控制和领域原生智能体。法律AI显示模型升级可抹平产品差距，而金融AI面临监管审视。
关键要点：

1. 成本感知的智能: 200美元套餐足以支撑出色工作；智能价格还会继续下降。
2. 路由与小模型: 按任务路由模型、直接操纵权重成为新的杠杆。
3. 智能体原生的垂直SaaS: 法律智能体竞争的是信任与工作流，而非仅模型访问。
启示：

1. 护城河转移: 不要建立在临时模型差距上；要掌握工作流、数据和信任。
2. 监管风险: 金融AI面临执法；合规将成为差异化优势。
总结：胜出的技术栈是更便宜的Token、更聪明的路由和领域信任，而不只是更大的模型。
