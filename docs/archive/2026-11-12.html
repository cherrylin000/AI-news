🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

From Flashy Demos to Co-Working Agents

Today's signal is clear: leading AI products are moving from one-shot demos to collaborative, embedded workflows, while the underlying model race (Gemini 4 Carbon, GPT-6 Astra) keeps accelerating.
Key Points:

1. Collaboration Beats Demos: Builders call for tools that let users co-create with models, not just watch one-shot showcases.
2. Embedded Into Tools: Claude's connectors, Data and Sheets push AI directly into Adobe, Atlassian, Box and analytics.
3. Model Race Heats Up: Gemini 4 Carbon ships to Ultra users while GPT-6 Astra surfaces in security audits.
4. Agent-Ready Infra Wins: Resend reached 4.3M users and $10M ARR by owning the developer email layer.
Implications:

1. Design for Co-Creation: Product teams should prioritize iterative, in-workflow collaboration over flashy one-shot demos.
2. Security Budgets Will Grow: AI-powered audits surfacing thousands of vulnerabilities will reshape security spend and review loops.
3. Own the Developer Layer: Developer-first infrastructure remains a durable wedge in crowded markets.
Bottom line: The winners will be tools that work alongside users inside real workflows—not just impress in a demo.

从炫技演示到共事智能体

今日信号明确：头部AI产品正从一次性演示转向协作式、嵌入工作流的形态，同时底层模型竞赛（Gemini 4 Carbon、GPT-6 Astra）持续加速。
关键要点：

1. 协作胜过演示: 开发者呼吁打造让用户与模型共同创作的工具，而非一次性展示。
2. 嵌入工具场景: Claude的连接器、Data与表格把AI直接放进Adobe、Atlassian、Box和分析场景。
3. 模型竞赛升温: Gemini 4 Carbon向Ultra用户开放，GPT-6 Astra则现身安全审计。
4. 智能体基础设施胜出: Resend凭开发者邮件层做到430万用户和千万美元ARR。
启示：

1. 为共创而设计: 产品团队应优先打造工作流内的迭代协作，而非炫目的一次性演示。
2. 安全预算将上升: AI审计曝出成千上万漏洞，将重塑安全支出与复核流程。
3. 抢占开发者层: 开发者优先的基础设施仍是拥挤市场中稳固的切入点。
总结：真正的赢家，是能嵌入真实工作流、与用户并肩协作的工具，而非只在演示中惊艳。
