1. Developer Costs: Agents made software cheap but coding expensive; Replit partners with OpenAI to address this.
2. Enterprise Privacy: OpenAI's Private Safety Processing enables Zero Data Retention and keeps customer data on their infrastructure.
3. Software Factories: AI promises to make software creation reliable for SMBs, solving chronic delays and budget overruns.
4. Narrative AI: Tolan shows AI as a new storytelling medium, where prompts are canvases and AI is an improv actor.
Implications:

1. For Enterprises: Zero-data-retention AI services can become a key differentiator for enterprises with strict data governance.
2. For Developer Tooling: Partnerships between AI labs and coding platforms will likely lower costs and democratize software creation.
3. For Content Creation: AI apps that enable interactive storytelling may find profitable niches beyond traditional media.
Bottom line: AI is moving from isolated tools to integrated platforms, balancing innovation with enterprise trust, and creating new consumer experiences.
