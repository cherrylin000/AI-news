🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Agents Embedded in Workflows and Scientific Loops

Builders are moving beyond agent hype toward interfaces and embedded workflows: generative UI and MCPs inside creator tools. In AI for science, the frontier shifts from mapping and prediction to steering experiments.
Key Points:

1. Generative UI as HAI: Rauch argues LLM-to-UI on Slack/Teams will become the dominant human-agent interaction paradigm.
2. MCPs inside creator apps: Thariq likes built-in MCPs in Instagram Edits and Roblox Studio, letting agents assist while power users continue in familiar tools.
3. From prediction to steering: Theis describes Lab Compass as an inverse generative model that guides the next experiment; the vision is RL in the loop.
Implications:

1. Design the interface, not just the agent: Embedded UI and MCP integrations lower adoption friction and keep humans in existing workflows.
2. Close the scientific loop: AI bio value shifts to models that recommend interventions and learn from wet-lab results.
Bottom line: The winning pattern is agents embedded in human workflows and scientific loops—not standalone chat.

智能体嵌入工作流与科学闭环

构建者正从智能体炒作转向界面与嵌入式工作流：生成式UI与创作者工具内置MCP。AI for science前沿则从图谱和预测转向引导实验。
关键要点：

1. 生成式UI成为人机交互: Rauch认为Slack/Teams上的LLM直达UI将成为主流人机交互范式。
2. 创作者工具内置MCP: Thariq看好Instagram Edits、Roblox Studio内置MCP，智能体辅助、高级用户在熟悉工具中续作。
3. 从预测走向引导: Theis称Lab Compass作为逆生成模型指导下一次实验，愿景是整个闭环RL化。
启示：

1. 不只做智能体，更要做界面: 嵌入式UI与MCP集成降低采用门槛，让人类留在既有工作流中。
2. 闭合科学实验环路: AI生物价值转向能推荐干预并从湿实验结果中学习的模型。
总结：胜出模式是嵌入人类工作流与科学闭环的智能体，而非孤立的聊天机器人。
