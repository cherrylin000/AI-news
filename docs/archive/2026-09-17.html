🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Agentic Infrastructure Meets Enterprise AI Diffusion

Builders are shipping integrations, stateless tool protocols, and probability-based models while enterprise AI moves from back office to growth workflows.
Key Points:

1. Tooling Shift: MCPs are favored over CLIs for integrations as models improve tool calling and MCP becomes stateless.
2. New Model Outputs: A new foundation model outputs probabilities, enabling faster and cheaper judging than Fable-level models.
3. Enterprise Expansion: Claude adds Salesforce and SMB workflows/integrations, reaching 900k installs and 43 workflows.
Implications:

1. Build for Composability: Design tools with query/filter params and defer loading to fit agentic workflows.
2. Cost Curves Matter: Probability-based judges can reshape eval and routing economics.
3. SMB Is the Next AI Frontier: Ready workflows and native integrations drive adoption beyond tech companies.
Bottom line: The stack is consolidating around agent-friendly protocols, cheaper inference, and enterprise-ready workflows.

智能体基础设施遇上企业 AI 扩散

构建者正交付集成、无状态工具协议和概率型模型，企业 AI 正从后台办公走向增长工作流。
关键要点：

1. 工具链转向: 随着模型工具调用增强和 MCP 无状态化，集成场景中 MCP 比 CLI 更受青睐。
2. 新模型输出: 新基础模型输出概率，可作为比 Fable 级模型更快、更便宜的评判器。
3. 企业扩展: Claude 新增 Salesforce 和中小企业工作流/集成，安装量达 90 万、工作流达 43 个。
启示：

1. 为可组合性构建: 工具应支持查询/过滤参数并延迟加载，以适配智能体工作流。
2. 成本曲线关键: 概率型评判器可能重塑评估与路由的经济性。
3. 中小企业是下一前沿: 现成工作流和原生集成将推动 AI 走出科技公司。
总结：技术栈正围绕智能体友好协议、更廉价推理和企业级工作流整合。
