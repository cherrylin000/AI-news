🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Agents Get Cheaper, More Persistent, and More Personal

Today's feed points to three converging shifts: inference economics improving (Sonnet 5.5 cache pricing halved), interfaces becoming generated on demand (ChatGPT custom UI), and persistent proactive agents entering real workflows via OpenAI's Dots.
Key Points:

1. Cost Curve Bends Downward: Halving cache reads to $0.10/M tokens cuts long-running agent workloads ~20%, making persistent agents economically viable.
2. UI Becomes an Output: ChatGPT generating custom UIs signals software interfaces shifting from hand-built to model-generated per task.
3. Persistent Agents Find Product-Market Fit: Every's team uses Dots to triage school emails, Slack access requests, and video edits hands-free — noise filtering is the killer use case.
4. Prompting Skill Is the New Bottleneck: Thariq notes failures cluster where users work outside their expertise and cannot be precise in prompts and plans.
Implications:

1. Build for Long-Running Work: Falling inference costs reward architectures built around memory, caching, and multi-turn persistence rather than single-shot calls.
2. Design for Generated Interfaces: Product teams should prepare for surfaces that assemble themselves per user and per task, and rethink design systems accordingly.
3. Teach Precision, Not Prompts: The leverage is domain context and crisp problem framing — teams should invest in evaluation loops, not prompt folklore.
Bottom line: The frontier is shifting from smarter single responses to cheaper, persistent, self-assembling agents — and the operators who can be precise will capture most of the value.

智能体更便宜、更持久、更个性化

今日信号指向三重交汇：推理成本下降（Sonnet 5.5 缓存读取减半）、界面按需生成（ChatGPT 定制 UI）、以及以 OpenAI Dots 为代表的持久主动式智能体进入真实工作流。
关键要点：

1. 成本曲线下探: 缓存读取减半至每百万 token 0.10 美元，长时智能体任务成本降约 20%，让持久化智能体具备经济可行性。
2. 界面成为产出物: ChatGPT 生成定制 UI，意味着软件界面正从手工构建转向按任务由模型即时生成。
3. 持久智能体找到产品契合点: Every 团队用 Dots 处理学校邮件、Slack 权限申请与视频剪辑，免手操作；过滤噪音是杀手级场景。
4. 提示能力成为新瓶颈: Thariq 指出失败多集中在用户专业领域之外——无法给出精准提示与计划。
启示：

1. 为长时任务而构建: 推理成本下降，将奖励围绕记忆、缓存与多轮持久化的架构，而非单次调用。
2. 为生成式界面做设计: 产品团队需准备好「界面按用户、按任务自组装」，并重新思考设计系统。
3. 教精准，而非教提示词: 真正的杠杆是领域上下文与清晰的问题定义，团队应投入评估闭环而非提示词玄学。
总结：前沿正从「更聪明的单次回答」转向「更便宜、更持久、能自我组装界面的智能体」，而能把问题定义精准的人将获取最大价值。
