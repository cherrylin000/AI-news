🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Agents Shift From Demos to Durable, Cost-Aware, Continuously Learning Systems

Today's builder signals show agent infrastructure maturing: Vercel ships durable workflows, Replit scales a viral AI-built page, and cost benchmarks favor Opus 5.5. Practical AI skills reach healthcare, while Trajectory pushes continual learning from batch updates to online loops.
Key Points:

1. Durable agent workflows: Vercel Workflow SDK 5 improves step latency, security, scale reliability, and reusable durability logs.
2. Cost-aware model selection: Opus 5.5 matches Fable 5.2 on coding tasks at much lower cost per solved task, making default choice matter.
3. Continual learning and real-world skills: Trajectory targets train-eval-deploy loops; Peter Yang shows AI skills solving concrete healthcare workflows.
Implications:

1. Build for reliability first: Agent products need durable execution, security, and disaster recovery before scaling.
2. Benchmark cost per outcome: Teams should track cost per solved task, not just raw accuracy, when choosing coding models.
3. Close the data loop: Winning products will turn production traces into continuous model improvement.
Bottom line: The agent stack is consolidating around durable execution, outcome-based cost efficiency, and closed-loop learning—while practical AI skills move into high-stakes domains.

智能体从演示走向可靠、成本敏感、持续学习的系统

今日 Builder 信号显示智能体基础设施正走向成熟：Vercel 推出持久化工作流，Replit 扩展爆款 AI 页面，成本基准倾向 Opus 5.5；实用 AI 技能进入医疗，Trajectory 推动持续学习从批量更新走向在线闭环。
关键要点：

1. 持久化智能体工作流: Vercel Workflow SDK 5 改善步骤时延、安全、规模化可靠性与可复用持久化日志。
2. 成本敏感的模型选择: Opus 5.5 在编码任务上媲美 Fable 5.2，但每个解决任务成本低得多，默认选择很关键。
3. 持续学习与真实场景技能: Trajectory 瞄准训练-评估-部署闭环；Peter Yang 展示 AI 技能解决具体医疗工作流。
启示：

1. 优先构建可靠性: 智能体产品在扩展前需要持久执行、安全与灾难恢复能力。
2. 按结果衡量成本: 选择编码模型时应追踪每个解决任务的成本，而非只看原始准确率。
3. 闭合数据闭环: 胜出的产品会把生产轨迹转化为持续的模型改进。
总结：智能体栈正围绕持久执行、基于结果的成本效率与闭环学习整合，同时实用 AI 技能进入高风险领域。
