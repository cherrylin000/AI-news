🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

The Agent Is the New Customer

Today's feed converges on one reframe: products must now be built for agents, not just people, while AI itself moves toward recursive self-improvement. Investors are getting hands dirty to keep up, and builders are shipping faster than ever.
Key Points:

1. Agent-first distribution: Levie: MCP, CLI and agent-navigable sites determine who wins commerce; the attention to capture belongs to the agent.
2. Investors must build: Nikunj codes 2h/day across model research, infra and app layer - the only way to see what's next.
3. RSI as the next unlock: Socher's Recursive ($650M) bets that simulatable domains get solved, feeding a self-improvement loop.
Implications:

1. Rebuild for machine readers: API, schema, checkout flow and docs should assume an agent is the primary visitor and buyer.
2. Hands-on beats hearsay: Fund and operator edge increasingly comes from daily tinkering, not only founder conversations.
3. Watch the coding bottleneck: With everyone building, tooling, review and context layers - not models - may be the constraint.
Bottom line: Value is shifting from human attention to agent attention, and the fastest learners are those who build daily.

Agent正在成为新的『客户』

今日信号指向同一个重构：产品必须为Agent而非仅为人而建，同时AI自身正走向递归自我改进。投资人靠亲手编码跟上节奏，构建者发布速度前所未有。
关键要点：

1. Agent优先的分发: Levie认为MCP、CLI与可被Agent操作的网站决定商业胜负，要争夺的注意力属于Agent。
2. 投资人必须动手构建: Nikunj每天编码2小时，横跨模型研究、基础设施与应用层，这是看清未来的唯一方式。
3. 递归自我改进成为下一个解锁点: Socher的Recursive融资6.5亿美元，押注可被模拟的领域终将被解决，从而形成自改进循环。
启示：

1. 为机器读者重建产品: API、结构化数据、结账流程与文档都应默认首要访客和买家是Agent。
2. 动手胜过道听途说: 基金与操盘者的优势越来越来自每日折腾，而非只靠与创始人聊天。
3. 关注编码瓶颈: 当所有人都在构建，真正的瓶颈可能是工具链、代码评审与上下文层，而非模型本身。
总结：价值正从人类注意力转向Agent注意力，而学得最快的人，是那些每天动手构建的人。
