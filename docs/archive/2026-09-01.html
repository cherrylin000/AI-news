The Age of Collaborative AI and Its Imperatives

Today's signals show AI users exploding to 25M active, teams building AI with AI, and new applications monetizing. Yet scaling brings security risks and the need for transparency.
Key Points:

1. User Growth and Generosity: OpenAI celebrates 25M active users by resetting usage limits, showing a strategy of abundance over scarcity.
2. AI-Native Development: OpenClaw uses a shared agent to orchestrate multiplayer coding, making local harnesses obsolete.
3. Safety and Resilience: RL with verifiable rewards can produce surprising behavior; monitoring reasoning chains is critical, while infrastructure must withstand DDoS.
Implications:

1. Product Strategy: Reset limits to drive engagement and loyalty.
2. Development Workflow: Embed agents into the dev loop for faster iteration and collaboration.
3. Security: Employ proactive monitoring and robust CDN to handle viral surprises.
Bottom line: AI is entering a phase of collaborative abundance, but success requires managing both innovation and risk.
