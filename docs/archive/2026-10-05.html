🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

Compute Scales Exponentially, Product Judgment Becomes the Scarcity

Today's signal is a widening gap: the infrastructure layer beneath AI is compounding at a pace that shocks even its builders, while the products built on top converge on the same features, aesthetics and metrics. Meanwhile, OpenAI's CEO flags AI-as-religion as a genuine safety issue, and OpenAI's own roadmap turns toward simplification rather than more surface area.
Key Points:

1. Data Layer Demand Outruns Forecasts: VAST Data's CEO describes a customer jumping from 500PB to 2 extra exabytes of planned demand — the hidden data layer is where AI capex actually lands.
2. Consolidation Over Feature Bloat: Claude merges Cowork and chat into one surface; OpenAI says it only ships simplifications, efficiency, breakthroughs or new models. Fragmentation is now a cost, not a feature.
3. Convergence Risk Is Real: Ryo Lu argues that when everyone copies what works and outsources judgment to AI, output becomes 'plausible, easy to approve' and interchangeable.
Implications:

1. Infra Players Stay Under the Radar: The largest value capture may sit in storage, networking and data orchestration — not in the models everyone benchmarks.
2. Design as Differentiation: As generation becomes cheap, the moat shifts to intention: knowing what deserves to exist and why this particular shape.
3. Safety Framing Widens: Expect 'AI as religion' and surrender-of-judgment to enter mainstream AI safety discourse alongside alignment and misuse.
Bottom line: The compute and data buildout is still accelerating without restraint, but the durable advantage is migrating from what you can generate to what you choose to make — and who is accountable for that choice.

算力指数级扩张，产品判断力成为稀缺资源

今日信号是一条正在扩大的鸿沟：AI底层基础设施以连建设者都震惊的速度复利增长，而构建其上的产品却在功能、审美与指标上趋同。与此同时，OpenAI CEO把“AI宗教化”列为真实安全问题，OpenAI自身路线也转向简化而非增加功能面。
关键要点：

1. 数据层需求超出预测: VAST Data CEO描述客户从500PB跳到再增2EB的计划需求——隐藏的数据层才是AI资本开支真正落地之处。
2. 整合优先于功能堆砌: Claude把Cowork与聊天合并为统一入口；OpenAI称只做简化、提效、突破性功能或新模型。碎片化已是成本，而非卖点。
3. 趋同风险确凿存在: Ryo Lu认为，当所有人复制成功者、把判断外包给AI，产出就变得“看似合理、容易通过”且可相互替换。
启示：

1. 基础设施玩家仍在雷达之下: 最大的价值捕获可能位于存储、网络与数据编排层，而非人人都在跑分的模型层。
2. 设计成为差异化来源: 当生成变得廉价，护城河转向意图：知道什么值得存在、为什么是这个特定形态。
3. 安全议题边界扩大: “AI宗教化”与“交出判断力”将与对齐、滥用并列，进入主流AI安全讨论。
总结：算力与数据建设仍在无节制加速，但持久优势正从“你能生成什么”迁移到“你选择做什么”，以及谁为这个选择负责。
