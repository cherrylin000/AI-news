🔥 Today's Top Takeaway

From Model Capability to Guidance and Intelligence Economics

Today's signal is less about bigger models than about how builders steer them, price intelligence, and protect the human parts of learning and collaboration.
Key Points:

1. Guidance is the #0 skill: Rauch says over- and under-guiding AI are both failure modes; managing that balance is the top skill.
2. Intelligence per dollar: Jev's Diogo Almeida argues cost, not raw intelligence per second, will make AI ubiquitous and unlock new apps.
3. Human struggle as moat: Ryo Lu warns that frictionless agents may reduce the shared struggle that builds taste, judgment, and friendships.
4. Engineering language matters: Rauch sees model vernacular as debugging/audit language and asks whether productive English can be far smaller than 900 words.
Implications:

1. For builders: Optimize for steering loops, evals, and cost per outcome—not just model IQ.
2. For product teams: Design AI to create more reasons to engage with the world and other people, not fewer.
3. For AI media: Editorial teams around AI are maturing; expect more reporting and review layers as the field commercializes.
Bottom line: The winning AI products will pair powerful models with better human guidance, lower cost per useful decision, and deliberate space for human connection.

从模型能力转向引导力与智能经济学

今日信号不在于更大的模型，而在于构建者如何引导模型、为智能定价，并守住学习与协作中人的部分。
关键要点：

1. 引导力是第0项技能: Rauch认为过度引导与引导不足都是失败模式；管理这一平衡是最重要的技能。
2. 每美元智能: Jev的Diogo Almeida认为，成本而非每秒原始智能，将让AI普及并解锁新应用。
3. 人类挣扎是护城河: Ryo Lu警告，无摩擦的代理可能减少共同挣扎，而后者塑造品味、判断力与友谊。
4. 工程语言很重要: Rauch将模型词汇视为调试/审计语言，并追问高效英语是否远少于900词。
启示：

1. 对构建者: 优化引导闭环、评估与单次结果成本，而不只是模型智商。
2. 对产品团队: 设计AI时，应创造更多进入世界和接触他人的理由，而非更少。
3. 对AI媒体: AI编辑团队正在成熟；随着领域商业化，将出现更多报道与审阅层。
